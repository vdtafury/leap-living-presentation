04 / Responsive strategy

Designed for the device, not shrunk for it

Four widths were designed deliberately. The mobile layouts are not stacked desktop layouts — the tasks, the order and the reach change.

1440px · Desktop

Full canvas

Multi-column grids, side drawers, hover-available detail. Used for the advisory desk and buyer research.

1024px · Small desktop

Condensed grid

Columns reduce, drawers remain drawers, and the primary action stays in the first viewport.

834px · Tablet

Two columns, full tasks

Property grids settle at two columns; filters move to a sheet without losing state.

390px · Mobile

Thumb-first

Single column, sticky primary action, sheets instead of drawers, and every target inside reach.

05 / Next steps

From sign-off to build

The design is complete and self-consistent. What remains is confirmation and handover.

01

Review and confirm the scope

Walk the pages and states above with the team. Flag anything that should change before it becomes a development cost.

02

Confirm the responsive breakpoints

Approve the four widths as the build target so development does not have to decide layout questions on its own.

03

Hand over the asset set

Exports are named, sized and organised per breakpoint. The Figma file stays the source of truth for anything not shown here.

04

Build, then compare against this

This presentation becomes the acceptance reference: spacing, type, colour and state behaviour are all defined above.
